SportzMate – Account Deletion Request

SportzMate allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Request Account Deletion

Email us at:

support@sportzmate.com

Subject: Delete My SportzMate Account

Information to Include

  • Your registered email address
  • Your display name (optional)
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

What Happens After Your Request

Once we verify your request, we will permanently delete your account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account profile
  • Name and email address
  • Profile photo
  • Sports preferences
  • Device registration information
  • Push notification registration (FCM token)
  • Future access to your account

Data That May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where required by applicable law, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our legal obligations.

Processing Time

Deletion requests are normally processed within 7 business days.
